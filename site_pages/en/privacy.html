1. Information we collect and why

  • Contact form: we receive your name, email address and message. They are used only to reply to you and are not kept longer than necessary after the reply.
  • Visit records: WordPress.com, which hosts this blog, may automatically collect access records (pages visited, browser type and so on) for visitor statistics.
  • Visit analytics: this blog uses Google Analytics to collect visit statistics (pages viewed, time on page, approximate region, device and browser type and so on). Cookies are used in the process, and no information that identifies you personally (such as your name or email) is collected. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • This blog does not offer membership and does not collect sensitive information such as national ID numbers.

2. Cookies and advertising

  • This blog may display third-party advertising such as Google AdSense.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this blog and other websites.
  • You can read how Google uses advertising cookies in Google's advertising policies, and you can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings.
  • You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you refuse them, some features may not display correctly.

3. External services

  • To show charts in posts, this blog loads an external charting tool (Plotly). In the process, that service may receive access information.
  • External websites linked from posts follow their own privacy policies.

4. Sharing with third parties

Except where required by law, we do not sell or hand over the personal information we receive to anyone else.

5. Requests to check or delete

If you would like to check or delete a message you sent, please ask through the contact page. We will confirm and handle it.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page with the date of the change.
