What software platforms does Palantir offer?

Palantir is a US company classified in the Software – Infrastructure industry within the technology sector. It has 4,634 full-time employees and mainly builds and deploys a range of software platforms for government agencies and businesses.

Palantir offers four main software platforms, supporting work from counterterrorism investigations to enterprise data integration and the use of large language models. It supports intelligence agencies in the US, the UK and elsewhere, and in defense it runs Palantir Gotham, which helps sense battlefield conditions, support decisions and connect analysts with operators in the field.

It also offers Palantir Foundry, which acts as a central operating system so companies can bring their internal data together and analyze it. Palantir Apollo, which helps deploy software and updates smoothly across an organization, is another main product. More recently it has expanded through the Palantir Artificial Intelligence Platform, which taps open-source and commercial large language models (LLMs) to turn structured and unstructured data into an analytical framework.

Annual results and revenue from 2022 to 2025

The company's financial condition and growth can be seen in its annual income statements. From 2022 to 2025, Palantir moved out of losses and steadily increased its profits.

Palantir's revenue rose from $1.91 billion in 2022 to $4.48 billion in 2025, an increase of 134.8% in total (+32.9% a year on average).

In 2022 it had an operating margin of -8.5%, with an operating loss of $160 million and a net loss of $370 million. It turned profitable in 2023 with operating profit of $120 million, and in 2025 it reached operating profit of $1.41 billion and net income of $1.63 billion, with its operating margin rising to 31.6%.

How did the past maximum drawdown and recovery play out?

How much a share price has swung can be seen from the maximum drawdown (MDD: the percentage fall from the highest value to the lowest value).

Palantir's past maximum drawdown was -84.6%: the fall from its peak on January 27, 2021 to its bottom on December 27, 2022 took 699 days.

Had you put in 10 million won at the high on January 27, 2021, its value would have shrunk to 1.54 million won at the lowest price. It then took until October 3, 2024, 646 days after the bottom, to regain the peak price. The share price is currently -31.4% below its 10-year high.

How much Palantir is in major ETFs?

Besides buying the stock directly, you can invest in Palantir through ETFs that bundle many stocks. Buying directly puts 100% of your money into Palantir alone, while an ETF reflects only its weight, with the rest spread across other companies.

In XLK (State Street Technology Select Sector SPDR ETF), one of the leading US technology ETFs, Palantir's weight is 2.61%.

In money terms, putting 1 million won into XLK means about 30,000 won of it goes to Palantir. Weights inside each ETF shift a little every day with share price moves and regular rebalancing.
