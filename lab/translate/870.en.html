How big is SK hynix, and what does it do?

SK hynix is a semiconductor company in the technology sector, headquartered in Icheon, Gyeonggi Province, South Korea, with 47,639 full-time employees and a market value of 784.9 trillion won. It was founded in 1949 and changed its name from Hynix Semiconductor to SK hynix in March 2012.

🔸 Main products and scope of business

SK hynix researches, develops, makes, distributes and sells semiconductor devices in Korea, China, the rest of Asia, the United States, Europe and other global markets. Its products include DRAM used in server, graphics, mobile, PC and consumer memory, as well as NAND flash memory, SSDs and MCP products.

It also runs a foundry business and produces non-memory semiconductors. Its products are used in servers, networking, mobile devices, personal computers, consumer electronics and automobiles.

How did annual results and revenue change from 2022 to 2025?

From 2022 to 2025, SK hynix's revenue rose from 44.6 trillion won to 97.1 trillion won, an increase of 117.7%, or 29.6% a year on average. After revenue of 44.6 trillion won, operating profit of 6.8 trillion won (an operating margin of 15.3%) and net income of 2.2 trillion won in 2022, revenue fell to 32.8 trillion won in 2023 and the company swung to a loss, with operating profit of -7.7 trillion won (an operating margin of -23.6%) and net income of -9.1 trillion won.

🔸 The recovery and revenue growth

In 2024 it bounced back with revenue of 66.2 trillion won, operating profit of 23.5 trillion won (an operating margin of 35.5%) and net income of 19.8 trillion won. In 2025 it then recorded revenue of 97.1 trillion won, operating profit of 47.2 trillion won and net income of 42.9 trillion won, with its operating margin rising to 48.6%.

How far has the price fallen recently?

Had you invested 10 million won at the peak on June 22, 2026, its value would have shrunk to 3.76 million won at the lowest price on November 17, 2026. After peaking on June 22, 2026, the price fell for 148 days until reaching the bottom on November 17, 2026.

🔸 How far it fell from the peak and what it did to an investment

The maximum drawdown over this period (MDD: the percentage fall from the highest value to the lowest value, i.e. the size of the biggest loss over that period) was -62.4%. The current price is -61.9% below its 10-year high, and it has not yet regained its earlier peak price.

What stands out in its dividend record?

The 2025 dividend per share was 2,429 won, more than double the 900 won of the previous year, 2024. SK hynix's annual dividend per share went from 1,170 won in 2020 to 2,429 won in 2025, an average increase of 15.7% a year over five years. Its dividend yield at the current share price, however, is about 0.22%.

🔸 What to keep in mind when reading the dividend trend

The 15.7% average is largely driven by the last year, 2025, jumping from 900 won in 2024 to 2,429 won, so it comes out better than the usual year-to-year trend. Why 2025 was so much higher is not in this data.

Summary

Because the gap between the size of its results and its price swings is large, you cannot judge the share price from revenue growth alone.
